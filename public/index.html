Yoga Tips & Articles

1. Five Easy Yoga Poses for Beginners

Beginners can start with gentle poses such as Mountain Pose, Child's Pose, Cat-Cow Pose, Tree Pose and Cobra Pose.

Practice slowly, breathe naturally and avoid forcing your body into uncomfortable positions.


2. Benefits of Daily Yoga Practice

Regular yoga practice may support flexibility, balance, body awareness and relaxation. Start with a short session and gradually develop a routine.


3. Simple Morning Yoga Routine

Begin with gentle breathing, shoulder movements, Cat-Cow stretches and comfortable standing poses. Finish with a few minutes of relaxation.


4. Meditation Tips for Beginners

Choose a quiet place, sit comfortably and focus on your breathing. If your mind wanders, gently bring your attention back to your breath.
